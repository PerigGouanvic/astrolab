# Centaurs of the Saturn Region: Orbits, Physical Data, Mythology and Astrology

This document covers eleven named Centaurs with orbital periods between 20 and 100 years. Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune are included for reference. Bodies are listed in order of orbital period.

Compiled November 2026.

## How to read this document

- Distances are in astronomical units (AU). One AU is the mean Earth–Sun distance.
- "Closest to farthest" means perihelion to aphelion.
- Centaur periods marked "about" were calculated with Kepler's third law (period in years = semi-major axis in AU raised to the power 1.5). Periods stated without "about" come from the object's own page. Centaur orbits shift over decades because of the giant planets, so treat all values as approximate.
- Albedo is the fraction of light a surface reflects (0.04 = 4%, as dark as charcoal). Colour classes: RR = very red, BR = moderately red, neutral = grey.
- Tholins are reddish organic compounds formed when ices are exposed to radiation. An occultation is the passage of a body in front of a star; timing it reveals shape and rings.
- Astrological meanings for the Centaurs are recent proposals, mostly from the 1980s onward. They are not classical doctrine. Each claim is attributed to its author.

Main sources:

- **Orbits:** [Wikipedia list of Centaurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_centaurs_(small_Solar_System_bodies)).
- **Physical data:** each object's Wikipedia page, which cites the original studies.
- **Planets:** the [NASA Planetary Fact Sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/) and Wikipedia's [Planets in astrology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planets_in_astrology).
- **Centaur mythology and astrology:** mostly Zane B. Stein's [Chiron and Friends](https://zanestein.com/) website, which collects his work and quotes other astrologers, and Melanie Reinhart's writing.

## Okyrhoe (52872, found 1998)

### Orbit
- Average distance: 8.4 AU
- Closest to farthest: 5.8 to 10.9 AU
- Period: 24.12 years

### Physical data
- Discovered by Spacewatch at Kitt Peak on 19 September 1998.
- Size: mean diameter about 49 km (Johnston, 2008).
- Rotation: 8.3 h (Bauer et al., 2003).
- Albedo: 0.03, very dark (Dotto, Barucci and de Bergh).
- Notable: it brightened markedly in March and April 2008 near perihelion, which "could be a sign of sublimation of volatiles" (Trigo-Rodríguez et al., 2008).
- Orbital half-life: about 670,000 years (Horner, Evans and Bailey, 2004).
- Source: [Wikipedia, Okyrhoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/52872_Okyrhoe).

### Mythology
- The name means "swift running" or "fast flowing".
- Okyrhoe was the daughter of Chiron and the nymph Chariklo, and she had the gift of prophecy.
- She foretold that Asclepius would become a great healer, anger the gods, be struck down and be reborn as a god.
- She began to foretell that her immortal father would be granted death, and Apollo turned her into a mare for revealing the plans of the gods ([Zane Stein, Okyrhoe](https://zanestein.com/okyrhoe.htm)).

### Astrology
- **Discovery degree:** 24° Pisces 35', conjunct the fixed star 51 Pegasi. Stein links the body to Pegasus symbolism and calls it "a bridge between Jupiter and Saturn" ([Zane Stein, Okyrhoe](https://zanestein.com/okyrhoe.htm)).
- **Amable** (Transneptunian Astrology blog), quoted by Stein: "The danger of Okyrhoe is to make a false move because of its strong ability for anticipation."
- **Mark Andrew Holmes,** quoted by Stein: it "seems to indicate being criticized or attacked, being able to cope with attacks or unpopularity, ability to stand up for one's beliefs or principles" ([Zane Stein, Okyrhoe](https://zanestein.com/okyrhoe.htm)).
- **The Centaur Space** (2020, author not named on the excerpt) reads Okyrhoe as "flowing waters of prophecy".
  - It notes that Nostradamus, Edgar Cayce and Manly P. Hall were all born with Okyrhoe near 25° Pisces.
  - Cayce's first trance reading fell during his first Okyrhoe return ([The Centaur Space](https://centaurs.space/2020/04/14/cases-of-centaur-astro/)).
- Keywords: prophecy, anticipation, speed, standing firm under attack.

## Thereus (32532, found 2001)

### Orbit
- Average distance: 10.6 AU
- Closest to farthest: 8.5 to 12.7 AU
- Period: about 34.5 years

### Physical data
- Discovered by NEAT at Palomar on 9 August 2001.
- Size: 62 to 86 km depending on the study (Stansberry et al., 2005; Bauer et al., 2013; Duffard et al., 2014).
- Rotation: 8.335 h (Ortiz et al., 2003; Rabinowitz et al., 2007).
- Albedo: 0.059 to 0.0975. Colour class BR.
- Composition:
  - The surface is "compositionally heterogeneous", with water ice ranging from about 0% to 25% across regions.
  - The ice is mixed with olivine, tholins and amorphous carbon (Licandro and Pinilla-Alonso, 2005).
- Source: [Wikipedia, Thereus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/32532_Thereus).

### Mythology
- Two Centaurs bore the name.
- The one Stein focuses on, likely the one Phil Sedgwick had in mind when he proposed the name, is a bear hunter killed by Theseus in Ovid's Metamorphoses. Stein says this Thereus treated the animals with cruelty and disrespect.
- The other Thereus was killed by Heracles among the Centaurs who rushed Pholus' cave for the wine ([Zane Stein, Thereus](https://zanestein.com/thereus.htm)).

### Astrology
- **Zane Stein:** "Perhaps one thing this body shows us is how humanity suffers by totally disprespecting the earth's resources?" and "perhaps it shows where we can try to control someone's path, without considering what they want?"
- **Amable:** "This centaur has a strong ability to control and dominate rebellious elements like a film director (theatre), or a teacher in class with students uproar."
- **John Delaney:** "lurking, then pouncing" ([Zane Stein, Thereus](https://zanestein.com/thereus.htm)).
- Discovery degree: 3° Pisces 46'.
- Keywords: control, domination, the hunter, lying in wait, ecological disrespect.

## Echeclus (60558, also comet 174P, found 2000)

### Orbit
- Average distance: 10.7 AU
- Closest to farthest: 5.8 to 15.6 AU
- Period: about 35 years

### Physical data
- Size: about 60 km. Rotation: 26.8 h, slow for a Centaur. Albedo: 0.04.
- Classification history:
  - In 2001 Rousselot and Petit (Besançon Observatory) concluded it was not a comet.
  - A coma appeared in December 2005.
  - In early 2006 it received the comet designation 174P. It was the second comet after Chiron to be named as a minor planet ([Wikipedia, Echeclus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/60558_Echeclus)).
- Outbursts:
  - **30 December 2005,** at 13.1 AU from the Sun: a large fragment broke off, releasing "a great cloud of dust".
  - **June 2011,** at 8.5 AU: an outburst reported by Giovanni Sostero and Ernesto Guido.
  - **7 December 2017,** at 7.3 AU: it was 4 magnitudes brighter than expected.
- Composition: carbon monoxide detected in 2016 (Wierzchos, Womack and Sarid, 2017).
- Orbital half-life: about 610,000 years.
- Source: [Wikipedia, Echeclus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/60558_Echeclus).

### Mythology
- The name's origin is unclear. It may come from a Greek word meaning "glutinous" (sticky).
- Its only known myth is in Ovid's account of the wedding of Pirithous: "Why tell of Ampyx who drove his cornel-wood spear that had lost its tip into the opposing face of four-footed Echeclus?" ([Zane Stein, The Mountain Astrologer, issue 170, 2013](https://mountainastrologer.substack.com/p/the-centaurs-series-echeclus-part)).

### Astrology
Zane B. Stein, "The Centaurs Series: Echeclus", first published in The Mountain Astrologer issue 170, August/September 2013 ([Mountain Astrologer](https://mountainastrologer.substack.com/p/the-centaurs-series-echeclus-part)):

- **Discovery degree:** 16°54' Virgo, whose Sabian symbol is "A volcano in eruption".
- **Core theme:** "rejection of superficiality" and "being very true to what one is at the deepest, most basic part of oneself."
- **The process:** "The Echeclus process begins with discovering how you are not being true to yourself".
- **Also:** the "urge to put your own personal stamp on the world around you" and "a conflict of 'us versus them'".
- **Cycles:**
  - Stein links the roughly 35-year orbit to a 35-year climate cycle.
  - The first Echeclus return falls in the same year as the third Jupiter return, since three Jupiter cycles equal 35.58 years.

Keywords: eruption, cathartic release, authenticity, refusal of the superficial.

- Rotation: 26.5 h (Gutiérrez et al., 2001) or 26.82 h (Bauer et al., 2002).

- Source: [Wikipedia, Elatus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/31824_Elatus).

### Mythology

- Stein notes that Latin elatus can also mean "puff-up, proud of oneself, arrogant" ([Zane Stein, Elatus](https://zanestein.com/elatus.htm)).

### Astrology
- **Amable:** "Elatus has an incredible ability to escape from the most dificult situations" and "is more habitual that he flees before things get worse."
- **Phil Sedgwick:** "Excellent communication, articulate, precise."
- **Mark Andrew Holmes:** "Elatus seems to indicate expression, expressiveness, eloquence" ([Zane Stein, Elatus](https://zanestein.com/elatus.htm)).
- **Zane Stein:**
  - He notes the discovery degree, 5° Taurus 12', close to Chiron's discovery degree (3° Taurus 08').
  - He links Elatus to the puer aeternus (eternal youth) archetype, Jupiter–Uranus qualities, "dodging a bullet", and the risks of boastfulness and self-deception ([Zane Stein, Elatus](https://zanestein.com/elatus.htm)).
- Keywords: escape, eloquence, the wound passed on, pride.

## Chiron (2060, also comet 95P, found 1977)

### Orbit
- Average distance: 13.7 AU
- Closest to farthest: 8.5 to 18.9 AU
- Period: about 50.7 years

### Physical data
- Discovered by Charles Kowal on 1 November 1977, from Palomar plates taken on 18 October.
- Size and shape: about 252 × 218 × 136 km, mean diameter 196 ± 34 km (2023).
- Mass and density: (4.8 ± 2.3) × 10¹⁸ kg; density 1.119 g/cm³ (2025 study).
- Rotation: 5.918 h.
- Albedo: about 0.15 to 0.16.
- Colour: neutral, similar to the nucleus of Halley's Comet.
- Composition: in 2024 the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) detected carbon dioxide, carbon monoxide, ethane, propane, acetylene and amorphous water ice.
- Comet-like activity:
  - **1988:** it brightened by 75%.
  - **April 1989:** coma observed by Karen J. Meech and Michael J. S. Belton.
  - **1991:** cyanide (CN) detected.
  - **1993:** tail detected.
  - **1995:** carbon monoxide detected.
- Rings:
  - Suspected from occultations in 1993, 1994 and 2011 (Ortiz et al., 2015).
  - Occultations in 2018 and 2022 show an evolving system, with a third ring fully formed by December 2022.
  - J. L. Ortiz suggested material from a 2021 outburst fed it.
- Future: it may become a short-period comet in about one million years.
- Source: [Wikipedia, Chiron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2060_Chiron).

### Mythology
- Chiron was the wise, immortal Centaur who taught heroes, including Achilles, in his cave on Mount Pelion ([Zane Stein, Pelion](https://zanestein.com/pelion.htm)).
- He was wounded by a poisoned arrow of Heracles during the brawl over Pholus' wine. Though immortal, he eventually traded his life for the freedom of Prometheus ([Zane Stein, Pholus](https://zanestein.com/pholus.htm)).

### Astrology
- **Melanie Reinhart,** Chiron and the Healing Journey (Penguin, 1989; revised edition with material on the Chiron return):
  - The book is described as revealing "Chiron's potential for distilling wisdom from our greatest suffering" ([book description, Bookbot](https://bookbot.de/g/8512584)).
  - From the book's cover text: "Suffering is present in everyone's life, but relating with wisdom and compassion to our own experience turns 'poison into medicine'" ([Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/21707368)).
- **Melanie Reinhart,** interviewed by Chris Brennan, The Astrology Podcast episode 271 ([transcript](https://theastrologypodcast.com/transcripts/tap-ep-271-transcript-the-astrology-of-chiron-with-melanie-reinhart/)):
  - "Chiron goes looping through and connecting the visible and the invisible, in a process of spiritually maturing".
  - A Chiron gift "is usually a gift that has been bestowed on the back of a lot of suffering."
- **Reinhart's framework:**
  - She links Chiron, Pholus and Nessus as mediators of Saturn/Uranus, Saturn/Neptune and Saturn/Pluto respectively.
  - She distinguishes people born when Chiron is inside Saturn's orbit from those born when it is outside ([CPA London](https://cpalondon.com/books/saturn-chiron-and-the-centaurs/)).
- **Zane B. Stein,** founder of the Association for Studying Chiron (1979 to 1986) and author of Essence and Application: A View From Chiron ([Zane Stein, bio](https://zanestein.com/bio.htm)):
  - Chiron is "a major key to understanding, not only our solar system (in astronomy), but healing and wholemaking (in astrology)" ([Zane Stein, Chiron](https://zanestein.com/chiron.htm)).
  - His keyword list includes "healing/wholemaking" and "maverick" ([Zane Stein, keywords](https://www.zanestein.com/keywords.htm)).
- Keywords: wounded healer, teacher, the bridge between Saturn and Uranus, the maverick, the Chiron return at about age 50.

## Chariklo (10199, found 1997)

### Orbit
- Average distance: 15.8 AU
- Closest to farthest: 13.2 to 18.5 AU
- Period: about 63 years

### Physical data
- Discovered on 15 February 1997 by Spacewatch, with James V. Scotti making the discovery observations.
- Size and shape: about 288 × 270 × 198 km, mean about 250 km. It is the largest known Centaur and is not in hydrostatic equilibrium.
- Mass and density: (5.9 to 6.9) × 10¹⁸ kg; density 0.73 to 0.85 g/cm³ (estimated, not measured).
- Rotation: 7.004 h (measured in 2013).
- Albedo: 0.037, very dark. Colour: reddish, BR.
- Composition: water ice, silicates, amorphous carbon and tholins. JWST (2022) confirmed crystalline water ice.
- Rings:
  - It was the first minor planet found to have rings, during an occultation on 3 June 2013 (Braga-Ribas et al., 2014).
  - The two rings, Oiapoque (radius about 386 km) and Chuí (about 400 km), are about 14 km apart.
  - Without shepherd moons, the rings "should disperse over a period of at most a few million years".
  - JWST observed a ring occultation on 18 October 2022.
- Orbital stability: a 50% chance of leaving the Centaur region in about 7 to 10.3 million years. Uranus mainly shapes its orbit.
- Source: [Wikipedia, Chariklo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10199_Chariklo).

### Mythology
- Chariklo was Chiron's wife, usually a nymph (sometimes shown as a female Centaur), said to be Apollo's daughter and a close companion of Athena.
- Her son Tiresias saw Athena naked, was blinded and received the gift of prophecy.
- Her daughters include Okyrhoe and, in some sources, Melanippe ([Zane Stein, Chariklo](https://zanestein.com/chariklo.htm)).
- The name means "Graceful Spinner" ([Melanie Reinhart, Chariklo](https://www.melaniereinhart.com/melanie/Chariklo.htm)).

### Astrology
- **Melanie Reinhart** ([Chariklo page](https://www.melaniereinhart.com/melanie/Chariklo.htm)):
  - "Perhaps we can imagine her being the one who cared for Chiron during his long life dealing with a wound he could not heal".
  - "Chariklo is a midwife." She describes a "Gift for creating or holding 'sacred space'".
  - Summary: "To 'bear with', abide with, create sacred space or appropriate boundaries, to accompany and bear witness."
  - She notes that Chariklo moves between Saturn and Uranus without crossing either orbit, "embodying their synthesis and integration".
- **Zane Stein** ([Chariklo page](https://zanestein.com/chariklo.htm)):
  - "Chariklo teaches us to question our perspective on reality", "she helps us get past blocks to loving", and "With Chariklo, I'm finding a choice to open a door."
  - Discovery degree: 6° Leo 04' retrograde.
- **Astrodienst article** (2020): "An astrological keyword for Chariklo is charisma, or the bestowing of grace" ([Astrodienst](https://www.astro.com/astrology/opa_article200219_e.htm)).
- Keywords: sacred space, midwifery, witnessing, grace, receptive strength.

## Bienor (54598, found 2000)

### Orbit
- Average distance: 16.5 AU
- Closest to farthest: 13.2 to 19.8 AU
- Period: about 67 years

### Physical data
- Size and shape: very elongated, about 254 × 110 × 90 km. A November 2024 occultation suggests a maximum length of about 275 km.
- Density: estimated at 0.55 to 1.15 g/cm³, likely about 0.7.
- Rotation: 9.1736 h. Albedo: 0.065. Colour: grey, neutral.
- Composition:
  - About 13% crystalline water ice in 2009, with the rest tentatively organic.
  - JWST (2024) found no recognizable spectral features.
- Possible ring: proposed in 2017 by a team led by Estela Fernández-Valenzuela, but not detected in occultations from 2019 to 2024.
- Source: [Wikipedia, Bienor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/54598_Bienor).

### Mythology
- The name means "strong man".
- In the one myth Stein found, Bienor fights the Lapiths at the wedding of Pirithous. Theseus leaps onto his back and crushes his face and temples with a club ([Zane Stein, Bienor](https://zanestein.com/bienor.htm)).

### Astrology
- **Zane Stein:** with so little myth, "we'll have to discern Bienor's influence by studying it without preconceptions."
- **Amable:** "This Centaur is conected to the self-organization of elements in a chaotic system. That means sincronicity or unusual conections of destiny between people or objects" ([Zane Stein, Bienor](https://zanestein.com/bienor.htm)).
- Discovery degree: 3°30' Pisces.
- Keywords: strength, synchronicity, order emerging from chaos.

## Asbolus (8405, found 1995)

### Orbit
- Average distance: 18 AU
- Closest to farthest: 6.8 to 29.2 AU
- Period: about 76 years

### Physical data
- Discovered on 5 April 1995 by James Scotti and Robert Jedicke (Spacewatch).
- Size: about 66 to 85 km. Rotation: about 8.9 h.
- Albedo: 0.04 to 0.13 depending on the study. Colour class BR.
- Notable: in 1998 Hubble found a fresh impact crater less than 10 million years old that exposes brighter ice.
- Dynamics:
  - Orbital half-life about 860,000 years. Saturn controls its perihelion and Neptune its aphelion.
  - An encounter with Jupiter in about 2,700 years will make its later path unpredictable.
- Source: [Wikipedia, Asbolus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8405_Asbolus).

### Mythology
- The name comes from a Greek word for "soot".
- Asbolus was a seer who foresaw the Centaurs' disastrous battle with the Lapiths and tried in vain to dissuade them.
- He is also said to have led the Centaurs toward Pholus' cave, where the smell of wine dulled his gift. Heracles killed him with arrows ([Zane Stein, Asbolus](https://zanestein.com/asbolus.htm)).

### Astrology
- **Philip Sedgwick,** quoted by Stein: "his Centaur insists that one always, always must follow their intuition" and "Learning augury or reading the omens and totems of life restore Asbolus-like guidance" ([Zane Stein, Asbolus](https://zanestein.com/asbolus.htm)).
- **Stein's keyword list** ([Zane Stein, Asbolus](https://zanestein.com/asbolus.htm)):
  - "the light that shines in the darkness"
  - reading omens and tapping the zeitgeist (spirit of the times)
  - being blinded by mist or smoke
- Discovery degree: about 7° Libra 44' retrograde, Sabian symbol "A blazing fireplace in a deserted home".
- **Alex Miller** describes those with a strong Asbolus as able "to tap into the zeitgeist", noting that "Often their advice or input is rejected, as being ahead of its time" ([Alex's Asteroid Astrology](https://alexasteroidastrology.com/asbolus/)).
- Keywords: augury, intuition, the unheeded warning, foresight clouded by intoxication.

## Crantor (83982, found 2002)

### Orbit
- Average distance: 19.5 AU
- Closest to farthest: 14.0 to 24.9 AU
- Period: 85 years and 10 months

### Physical data
- Discovered by NEAT at Palomar on 12 April 2002.

  - A transient "horseshoe" co-orbital of Uranus, sharing its period (1:1 resonance).

- Stein wonders whether the body has "a Crantor side, and a Demoleon side" ([Zane Stein, Crantor](https://zanestein.com/crantor.htm)).

### Astrology
- **Amable:** "a sudden death or end can become in any moment for reasons beyond our control".

- Discovery degree: 7° Scorpio 26'.
- **Vamzzz blog** (author not named on the excerpt) reads Crantor as "the fragility of life and the fatality of death and how we deal with this contrast emotionally, psychologically, and on a mental and spiritual level" ([Vamzzz](https://vamzzz.com/blog/crantor-astrology/)).
- Keywords: sudden endings, vulnerability, the innocent caught in conflict.

## Pholus (5145, found 1992)

### Orbit
- Average distance: 20.3 AU
- Closest to farthest: 8.7 to 31.9 AU
- Period: about 91.5 years

### Physical data
- Size and shape: about 99 km (Herschel), elongated. Older estimates of up to 190 km relied on an outdated, lower albedo.
- Rotation: 9.98 h, with a large brightness amplitude of 0.60 magnitude (Tegler, 2005).
- Albedo: about 0.13 to 0.16.
- Colour: among the reddest objects known (Tholen class Z, RR), probably from surface organics.
- Composition (model):
  - Dark amorphous carbon plus an intimate mixture of water ice, methanol ice, olivine and tholins.
  - Very Large Telescope (VLT) polarimetry (2007 to 2008) indicates "a rather homogeneous surface with a small amount of water frost on its darker regions".
- Activity: "Unlike Chiron, Pholus has shown no signs of cometary activity."
- Source: [Wikipedia, Pholus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5145_Pholus).

### Mythology
- Pholus was a gentle, wise Centaur who hosted Heracles and opened, or allowed to be opened, the jar of wine.
- The smell drove the other Centaurs into a frenzy. Heracles' poisoned arrows wounded Chiron.
- Pholus died after pricking himself with an arrow he was pulling from a fallen Centaur ([Zane Stein, Pholus](https://zanestein.com/pholus.htm)).
- The jar belonged to Dionysus and "was not supposed to be opened for four generations" ([Melanie Reinhart, All Change](https://melaniereinhart.com/melanie/astrologyarticles/all-change.php)).

### Astrology
- **Robert von Heeren and Dieter Koch** (German research, mid-1990s), quoted by Stein:
  - "Chiron brings long and helpless waiting, while Pholus, on the other hand, brings immediate progress."
  - Von Heeren's tentative keywords include "awakening", "the unconscious takes control", "freeing, release" and "intensity, intensifier" ([Zane Stein, Pholus](https://zanestein.com/pholus.htm)).
  - The formula "small cause, big effect" is attributed to von Heeren ([Astroak](https://www.astroak.com/de/blog/pholus-im-geburtshoroskop)).
- **Melanie Reinhart,** "Pholus: the lid comes off" ([All Change](https://melaniereinhart.com/melanie/astrologyarticles/all-change.php)):
  - "a small event sets off a chain reaction, either inwardly or externally, in terms of consequences".
  - "the astrological Pholus often highlights ancestral processes, whether known or unknown to us, which are releasing".
- **Zane Stein:**
  - Stein proposes Pholus as a link between Saturn and Neptune, as Chiron links Saturn and Uranus.
  - He sees a theme of selflessness, "involving the self in something to the point of losing the self in the process" ([Zane Stein, Pholus](https://zanestein.com/pholus.htm)).
  - Discovery degree: 0° Leo 36' retrograde.
- **Marianne Alexander** wrote The Centaur Pholus (June 1996) ([Zane Stein, What are the Centaurs](https://zanestein.com/page4_1.htm)).
- Keywords: small cause with big effect, the lid coming off, release of ancestral patterns, intoxication, irreversibility.

## Neptune (reference)

### Orbit
- Average distance: 30.07 AU
- Closest to farthest: 29.8 to 30.3 AU
- Period: 164.8 years

### Physical data
- Diameter 49,528 km, mass 102 × 10²⁴ kg, density 1,638 kg/m³.
- Rotation 16.1 h, 16 moons, ring system ([NASA](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/)).

### Mythology
- Named after the Roman "god of the sea". Discovered in 1846 ([Wikipedia, Planets in astrology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planets_in_astrology)).

### Astrology
- Keywords: "the collective consciousness, the tenderness, the peace", "dreams/fantasy", "artistry, empathy" and "illusion/confusion/vagueness".
- Modern ruler of Pisces, exalted in Aquarius ([Wikipedia, Planets in astrology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planets_in_astrology)).

## Notes on the sources

- **Depth of literature.**
  - Chiron, Pholus and Chariklo have books or long articles by named authors: Reinhart, Stein, von Heeren and Koch, and Alexander.
  - Echeclus has a full article by Stein (The Mountain Astrologer, 2013).
  - The others (Okyrhoe, Thereus, Elatus, Bienor, Asbolus, Crantor, Pelion) rest mainly on short notes collected on Stein's website, with quotes from Amable, Philip Sedgwick, Mark Andrew Holmes and John Delaney.
- **Who "Amable" is.** Amable is the author of the Transneptunian Astrology blog, as cited by Stein. Stein's pages give no full name.
- **Dates.** Most of Stein's centaur pages carry no publication date, so the dates given here are discovery dates or the dates of the articles cited.
- **Quotations.** Quotations are reproduced exactly as they appear on the source pages, including original spelling errors.
- **Excluded bodies.** Nessus, Hylonome, Amycus and Cyllarus have periods of about 123 to 133 years and fall outside the 20 to 100 year window.
